Broccoli Salad

Description:

The last time my mother made broccoli salad it nearly killed me, so here is my better, non-poisonous recipe.

Ingredients:

  • 8 cups of broccoli florets cut into small pieces
  • 1/2 cup dried cranberries
  • 1/4 cup sunflower seeds
  • 1/2 cup bacon bits
  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • 3 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Directions:

  1. Whisk together mayonnaise, vinegar, sugar, salt and pepper in a medium bowl. Set aside.
  2. In a large bowl, combine broccoli, cranberries, sunflower seeds, and bacon bits. Pour the prepared dressing over and mix well.
  3. Refrigerate for at least one hour before serving.
