Macaroni and Cheese

Description:

This is the best mac n cheese recipe ever. In fact, I'd almost say it's killer. There is so much cheese it could probably stop your heart if you're not careful.

Ingredients

  • 12 ounces elbow macaroni
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 1 cup half and half cream
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • 10.75 oz condensed cheddar cheese soup (1 can)
  • 4 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 cup shredded parmesan cheese

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 425f.
  2. Cook macaroni according to package instructions. Drain under cold water.
  3. Melt butter over medium heat in a large saucepan.
  4. Whisk in flour and let cook 2 minutes while stirring.
  5. Slowly whisk in milk, salt and pepper.
  6. Cook over medium heat while stirring until thickened.
  7. Remove from heat and stir in parmesan cheese and 3 cups cheddar cheese until melted.
  8. Add soup if using.
  9. Toss cheese sauce and noodles together. Pour into greased 9x13 inch pan. Top with remaining 1 cup cheese.
  10. Bake 18 to 24 minutes or until bubbly. Do not overcook.
  11. Let cool for 10 to 15 minutes before serving.
