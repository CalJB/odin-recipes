Chocolate Chip Oatmeal Cookies

Description

This is my grandmother's top secret recipe. DO NOT READ IT!

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup shortening
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup white sugar
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/4 cup boiling water
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 2 cups rolled oats
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Directions:

  1. Cream shortening with white and brown sugar until light and fluffy.
  2. Dissolve baking soda in boiling water, then stir into creamed mix along with the vanilla.
  3. Combine flour, salt, and rolled oats in a separate bowl.
  4. Mix dry parts slowly into wet mixture until stiff dough forms.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Shape into small balls, place on an ungreased sheet, and flatten slightly.
  7. Bake at 350f for 12 to 15 minutes until set, avoiding over-browning.
